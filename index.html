Why Deutschlify?

A course that starts with your words.

Most apps hand you a fixed list. Deutschlify is built around the German you actually meet, and it tells you the truth about how well you know it.

01

Your vocabulary

Build collections from a textbook page, a photo, a PDF or a conversation. You decide what is worth learning.

02

Memory-aware review

A spaced-repetition schedule brings each word back shortly before you would forget it.

03

Honest numbers

Every chart is drawn from your own answers. No inflated streaks and no invented scores.

04

Private by default

Your vocabulary and progress are stored in your browser, on your own device.

Smart Vocabulary

More than a word and its translation.

German words carry an article, a plural and a set of forms. Each entry keeps them together, so you learn the whole word.

  • Complete entriesArticle, plural, verb forms, example sentences, synonyms and your own notes.
  • Add many at oncePaste a list such as “gehen - to go” and review every line before saving.
  • Import from a photo or PDFText is recognised on your device. Nothing is saved until you confirm it.
  • Statuses that mean somethingNew, Learning, Learned and Difficult are calculated from your answers.
